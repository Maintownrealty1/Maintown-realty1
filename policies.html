Policies

Policy & Transaction Guidelines

MainTown Realty is committed to providing a trusted, transparent, and structured housing experience for all users.

1. Verification Policy

All properties and participants within our system go through a structured verification process to reduce fraud and ensure credibility.

2. Payment Policy

  • Payments are only made after proper verification and confirmation
  • Users are guided through a secure and transparent payment process
  • MainTown Realty does not support unverified or off-platform transactions

3. Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on:

  • Stage of transaction
  • Nature of agreement
  • Verification outcome

Clear refund terms will be communicated before any payment is made.

4. Property Matching Policy

MainTown Realty does not operate as an open listing marketplace. We provide curated and verified property matches based on user requests.

5. Partner & Agent Policy

All agents, landlords, and service providers within our network are expected to operate under:

  • Transparency
  • Professional conduct
  • Verified engagement

6. User Responsibility

Users are expected to:

  • Provide accurate information
  • Follow the guided process
  • Avoid engaging in unofficial transactions outside the system

7. Transparency Commitment

We aim to ensure that:

  • Every step is clear
  • Every transaction is traceable
  • Every interaction builds trust

We exist to remove uncertainty.

MainTown Realty creates a safer way to access housing.
