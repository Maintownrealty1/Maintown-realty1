Smart Tips Before Securing a Property

Finding a home should be safe, clear, and stress-free. Use these tips to avoid common mistakes and make confident decisions.

1

Verify the Property

Always confirm that the property exists and is legitimately available. Avoid making payments for listings you have not verified.

2

Understand the Full Cost

Look beyond rent. Ask about:

  • Service charges
  • Caution fees
  • Maintenance costs

Make sure you know your total financial commitment upfront.

3

Inspect Before Payment

Where possible:

  • Visit the property physically
  • Or request a video inspection

Never rely only on pictures.

4

Avoid Direct Unverified Payments

Do not send money directly to unknown agents or landlords without proper verification.

5

Prioritize Security & Location

Check:

  • Power supply stability
  • Water availability
  • Security (gated estate, CCTV, neighborhood safety)
  • Accessibility (roads, transport)
6

Use Structured Platforms

Working with a structured system like MainTown Realty ensures:

  • Verified listings
  • Transparent process
  • Reduced risk

Your safety is more important than speed.

Always choose trust over urgency.
