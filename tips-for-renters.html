Tips for Renters & Buyers

Smart Tips Before Securing a Property

Finding a home should be safe, clear, and affordable. Use these tips to avoid common mistakes, understand your costs, and choose a payment plan that works for you.

1

Verify the Property Before You Pay

Confirm the property exists and is genuinely available. On MainTown, look for the Preliminary Verification tag — automated, early checks on the documents supplied for every listing. It is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of title, so do your own checks too.

  • Never pay for a listing you haven't verified yourself
  • Buying? Instruct an independent lawyer and run your own searches before you pay or sign
  • Buying land or property? Go for an inspection first — see it in person or virtually before you commit
2

Know Your Full Cost Upfront

Rent is only part of the picture. Ask for a clear breakdown of:

  • Agent and legal fees
  • Caution fees and service charges
  • Maintenance costs

On MainTown, agent and legal fees are itemized before you commit — no surprises later.

3

Choose a Payment Plan You Can Sustain

You don't have to fund a full year of rent upfront. MainTown offers monthly, quarterly and half-yearly plans. Before you accept one:

  • Check the payment schedule and due dates
  • Confirm what you'll pay in total over the term
  • Understand what happens if a payment is missed

Only commit to a plan your income can comfortably carry.

4

Inspect Before You Commit

Never rely on pictures alone. Where possible:

  • Visit the property physically
  • Or use the video walkthrough or request a video inspection
5

Pay Only Through the Platform

Don't send cash or transfers to personal accounts, and avoid off-platform deals — even if someone offers a discount.

  • Pay through MainTown's payment flow, processed by licensed payment partners
  • Rent goes to the landlord on schedule — MainTown does not hold your money
  • Keep every receipt and confirmation
6

Read and Keep Your Agreement

Read your tenancy or purchase agreement fully before signing. Pay attention to:

  • Rent schedule and renewal terms
  • Caution fee refund conditions
  • Notice periods and house rules

Your signed agreement is stored in your MainTown account so you can always find it.

7

Check the Neighbourhood

A good price isn't worth a bad location. Check:

  • Power supply stability
  • Water availability
  • Security (gated estate, CCTV, neighbourhood safety)
  • Accessibility (roads, transport)
8

Report Issues Early

Once you move in, report faults quickly with a photo or short video so they can be fixed properly.

  • Use Quick Fix for plumbing, electrical, AC, cleaning and more
  • Keep records of anything you report

Your safety is more important than speed.

Always choose trust over urgency.
