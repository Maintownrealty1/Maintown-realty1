Vision & Mission

Vision

"To transform housing accessibility across Africa and the diaspora by building an undeniable trust layer powered by AI — where upfront financial barriers are eliminated, and every property transaction is transparent, secure, and flexible."

Mission

MainTown Realty is on a mission to solve the rental and property ownership crisis starting in Nigeria and across the diaspora. By replacing traditional annual upfront rent with intelligent monthly payment structures, running automated, preliminary verification checks on property documents, and expanding access across emerging suburban hubs, we empower local tenants, landlords, and diaspora buyers with a seamless, trustworthy housing ecosystem.

About Us

Who We Are

MainTown Realty is Nigeria's verified property marketplace — built to make finding, renting, and buying a home as trustworthy as it should have been all along.

Housing in Nigeria has long asked too much of too many people — a full year's rent demanded upfront, agent fees with no clear breakdown, and no real way to confirm a listing is genuine before money changes hands. MainTown Realty exists to change that. We built a platform where every listing is verified, every fee is itemized before you commit, and every tenant, buyer, and landlord can transact with confidence.

What started as a simple idea — that finding a home shouldn't require blind trust — has grown into a full property ecosystem serving renters, buyers, landlords, and agents across Nigeria, with land and property purchase options now extending to diaspora Nigerians investing from abroad.
