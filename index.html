Vision & Mission

Vision

"To transform housing accessibility across Africa and the diaspora by building an undeniable trust layer powered by AI — where upfront financial barriers are eliminated, and every property transaction is transparent, secure, and flexible."

Mission

MainTown Realty is on a mission to solve the rental and property ownership crisis starting in Lagos, and Nigeria at large. By replacing traditional annual upfront rent with intelligent monthly payment structures, utilizing AI to verify property documents and prevent fraud, and expanding access across emerging suburban hubs, we empower local tenants, landlords, and diaspora buyers with a seamless, trustworthy housing ecosystem.
