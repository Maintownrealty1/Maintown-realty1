Bulk Import Listings

Add many listings at once, from a spreadsheet (CSV) or pasted JSON. Each one is saved exactly like the form below, and goes live on your site straight away.

In Excel or Google Sheets use File > Save as / Download > CSV. Put photo links in the images column, separated by a vertical bar ( | ). One row per listing.

Open it in Excel, replace the sample rows with yours, save as CSV.
Which columns does each listing type use?

All types: category (rent, commercial, land, short-let), title, location, price, features and images (separate with | ), video, status (available, sold, rented, booked, unavailable — unavailable hides the listing from the public site), extraFees (one per | ).

Rent (residential): price is the monthly rent, plus beds, cautionFee, serviceCharge and serviceChargeFreq (yearly or monthly). Agent Fee (10%) and Legal Fee (5%) are worked out for you from the yearly rent, exactly like the form. Fill annualRent only if the yearly rent is not monthly x 12.

Commercial: category commercial (or rent with usage = commercial). price is the annual rent. Uses commercialType (Shop, Office, Warehouse, Event Hall), sqm, parking, power (Public grid, Grid + generator, Grid + inverter/solar, Generator only), cautionFee, serviceCharge.

Land & Property: price is the outright price. propertyType (Land (Plot), Duplex, Bungalow, Detached House, Semi-Detached Duplex, Terrace, Block of Flats, Mansion). Houses also use beds, bathrooms, toilets, parking. Installments: installmentPrice, installmentMonths (3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24), initialDeposit. Sold in parts: soldInParts (yes/no), halfPlot (yes/no), partSize, plotsAvailable, halfPlotPrice and halfPlotInstallment (left empty they default to half). Costs: legalFeePercent (5 or 10, worked out for you), surveyPlan, redCopy. Also plotSize, titleDocument (C of O, Governor's Consent, Excision, Gazette, Registered Survey, Deed of Assignment) and landUse (Residential, Commercial, Mixed-Use, Agricultural).

Short Let: price is the nightly rate. beds, minStay, cleaningFee, cautionFee, serviceCharge, serviceChargeFreq, openFrom and openUntil (both or neither, YYYY-MM-DD), blockedDates (separate with | ), about.

Listings already on the site (same title and location) are skipped, so uploading the same file twice does not create doubles. Use Check only first to see any problems.
